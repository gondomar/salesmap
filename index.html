    Add someone to the app

    1. Add them to your team

    Open Menu → Team, enter their email under Add someone, and tap Add to team. Only owners can do this.

    Starting a separate business for someone else (like a relative with their own company)? Use Create another business on the Team screen instead. Only the app admin sees that option.

    2. Send them the link

    3. They create their account

    1. Open the link and tap Create account.
    2. Enter the same email you added, choose a password, and tap Create account.
    3. Open the confirmation email (check spam), tap the link, then go back to the app and tap Continue.

    Forgot a password? Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen.

    4. Put it on their home screen

    iPhone: open the link in Safari (not Chrome), sign in, tap the Share button (square with an up arrow), then Add to Home Screen → Add.

    Android: open the link in Chrome, sign in, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

    The first time they open it from the icon, they should tap Allow when it asks for location.
